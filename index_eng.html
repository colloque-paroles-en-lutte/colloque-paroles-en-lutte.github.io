“Reordering the World”:
Epistemologies, Law, and Struggles for Liberation

between april 1 - 17 | Paris

Deadline for registration: March 15th, 2026

About the Conference

This conference seeks to explore the ways in which criminologists can, and should, address state criminality; to consider how criminological scholarship may contribute critical analyses of violations committed by Israel in Palestine...

Paper proposal: Two streams

When submitting a paper proposal, authors should indicate their preference to participate in one of the following two streams:

  • Palestine: Criminological Perspectives – Exploring state crime and international law.
  • Academic Complicity – Addressing engagement and responsibility in atrocity crimes.

Practical Information

There is no conference registration fee. Participants who do not have institutional funding are invited to contact the committee for support.
